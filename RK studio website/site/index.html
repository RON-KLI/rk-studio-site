Ron Klimovsky Studio

Ron Klimovsky (a.k.a. ARC) is a multidisciplinary visual artist working mainly in the United States. The studio is the primary public surface for the work — paintings, drawings, editions, and a calendar of exhibitions. There is no agent and no waiting list; inquiries go directly to the studio at studio@ronklimovsky.com.

About

I grew up in a family of second-generation gallerists and saw the art world from the inside before deciding to make work myself. I approach art from first principles and am deliberately discreet about process and biography — the work itself is the medium for the ideas.

I work primarily in oil on linen, oil on panel, graphite on paper, charcoal on paper, and small-edition intaglio prints. Ongoing series include Rooms at dawn (interiors painted in the hour before the day begins) and Drawings (a daily graphite-and-charcoal practice).

Ron Klimovsky Studio is my way of financing the creation of art, so I can make more and greater art.
